Timeless Compass: Research Repository

Explore published research on ancient systems of knowledge.

Welcome to Timeless Compass

Curated by the Sri Sri Institute for Advanced Research (SSIAR), the research wing of the Art of Living, the Timeless Compass: Research Repository is an interactive digital repository of global studies on Ancient Systems of Knowledge (ASK) that connects ancient wisdom with modern scientific inquiry.

How to navigate: the repository is organized into two tabs, sharing one set of filters.

📊 Dashboard — Scorecards and visualizations offering a bird's-eye view of the repository: publication trends by year, research topics by volume, geographic distribution, and research partnerships. Click any chart to filter the studies — that filter carries straight over to the Database tab.

📋 Database — The full research table, useful for direct comparison and study selection. Search by keyword, or use the Advanced Search sidebar to narrow studies by topic, population, intervention, partnership, language, year, or country. Intervention is a multi-select — tick as many as you like and a study matching any of them will show. Click any row to open a study's full details, including a link to the original peer-reviewed work.

A note on AI use: This repository presents bibliographic and publication metadata as reported in the original sources. AI tools assisted in extracting this metadata, which was reviewed and validated by the SSIAR team; classification into partnership type, topic, and subtopic was carried out entirely by expert human review.

Finding Your Way Around

Start with what you want to know. Curious about what science has discovered? Looking for evidence on a specific question? Here's how to find it.

Two tabs, one set of filters. The Dashboard stands on its own — its charts double as filters. The Database tab adds a search bar and an Advanced Search sidebar. Whatever you set in one is already applied in the other; nothing resets when you switch tabs.

Explore by curiosity (the scenic route): Start on the Dashboard tab. Click "Mental Health" in the topics chart to see studies on stress, anxiety, or depression. Hover over the year chart to spot research peaks. Click a bubble on the map to explore studies from a specific country. Every click updates the shared filter state — switch to the Database tab any time to see the matching studies.

Search by specifics (the direct route): Switch to the Database tab and use the search bar for keywords — "anxiety in nurses," "heart disease," "brain imaging," or an author name. The Advanced Search sidebar lets you filter by topic, population, intervention, partnership, language, year, or country at the same time. The table updates live.

Intervention is multi-select. Click the Intervention field to open a checklist, tick as many interventions as you like, and click elsewhere to close it — a study matching any of your picks will show.

Combine filters to answer real questions: Select multiple filters to refine further. Want research on anxiety or depression in young people from the past five years? Set Topic, Population and Year in the sidebar. Every active filter appears as a removable chip above the results — click × to remove any, or use Clear All to start over.

Dive into individual studies: Click any row in the Database table to open a study's full details — findings, population, intervention, study design, author affiliation, and a link to the published paper.

Pro tip: Start on the Dashboard to understand the landscape, then move to the Database to target specific questions. Because both tabs share one filter state, you never lose your place moving between exploration and detail.
