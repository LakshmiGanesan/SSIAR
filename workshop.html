About the Workshop

Rigorous science, without erasing where the knowledge came from

Researchers working on yoga, Ayurveda, traditional medicine, and other ancient systems of knowledge face a distinct set of publishing challenges: how to credit knowledge-holding communities fairly, how to satisfy reviewers trained in a different evidentiary tradition, and how to write about a practice's cultural context without either exoticising it or stripping it out entirely.

This workshop, delivered in partnership with British Council India, brings together researchers, science writers, and community knowledge-holders to work through those questions directly — with real examples of papers that got this right, and ones that didn't. You'll leave with a practical framework for structuring your next paper, and a clearer sense of what ethical, consent-based collaboration with knowledge-holding communities looks like in practice.

Open to researchers, independent scholars, and practitioners at any career stage — no prior publishing experience required, and no specialised equipment beyond a laptop.
