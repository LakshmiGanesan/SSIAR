About the Symposium

Where contemplative tradition meets the laboratory

For two days, researchers, clinicians, and practitioners will gather to examine what a growing body of evidence is telling us about breath-based and meditative interventions — from cellular and genomic markers to outcomes in trauma recovery, cardiovascular health, and workplace resilience.

The programme opens with a keynote on the physiology of Sudarshan Kriya Yoga (SKY), followed by parallel tracks on mental health interventions, autonomic and immune function, and community-scale implementation. Each day closes with a moderated panel connecting the day's findings to real-world practice — for clinicians building protocols, and for policymakers weighing what belongs in public health guidance.

A dedicated poster session will showcase ongoing and early-stage studies from the wider ASK (Ancient Systems of Knowledge) research community, with informal mentorship slots for early-career researchers presenting for the first time.
