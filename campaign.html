The Cause

Burnout is a workforce crisis, not a personal failing

Nearly two in three frontline healthcare workers in India report symptoms of burnout — emotional exhaustion, detachment from patients, and a shrinking sense of accomplishment. The cost isn't just personal: burnt-out clinicians leave the workforce, make more errors, and report worse patient outcomes.

Small studies have shown that Sudarshan Kriya Yoga (SKY), a structured breathing practice, measurably lowers cortisol and improves markers of autonomic balance in as little as four days. What's missing is a properly powered, randomised trial in the population that needs it most: nurses and physicians working double shifts in under-resourced hospitals.

"We keep telling frontline staff to look after themselves. What we've never given them is evidence-backed, five-day-a-week support that fits inside a hospital shift." — Dr. Ananya Rao, Principal Investigator

Your contribution funds a 6-month randomised controlled trial across three partner hospitals: 240 participants, validated burnout and biomarker assessments at baseline, 8 weeks, and 6 months, and a published, open-access report so any hospital can adopt what works.
